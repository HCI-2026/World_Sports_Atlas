Photos

Photos are the main images of each sport's Wikipedia article, loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Where that image did not show the sport (a logo or a stadium), we chose a match photo from Wikimedia Commons instead. Each photo in a sport's panel links to its file page, which lists the photographer and licence.

Wikipedia has no photo of Nguni stick fighting, so that photo comes from the Face2Face Africa article on Zulu stick fighting and is saved in the site's images folder.
